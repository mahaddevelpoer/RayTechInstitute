# Educational resource backlink strategy

Status: prepared locally on 26 October 2026. No outreach, account edits, submissions or backlink purchases have been performed. Publishing requires the owner's explicit approval.

## Before outreach

1. Buy and connect raytechinstitute.com, complete Firebase HTTPS and verify the actual canonical domain. Keep the current working domain until this is complete.
2. Publish the approved changes, verify public resource URLs and submit the canonical sitemap in Search Console.
3. Check that each promoted resource has a clear class, subject, topic, language and source. Do not describe a course as complete when its remaining lessons have not been written.

## Resources worth linking to

- The D.I.T Computer Science Chapter 1 question bank: eight topics with ten English and Urdu MCQs each, plus the matching revision PDF and answer key.
- Individual programming lessons that include a runnable example, practice activity and topic test.
- Notes and past papers with accurate document context and working previews/downloads.
- Board guidance that links to the original issuing institution and distinguishes confirmed notices from unverified claims.

## First links to improve

The website's own footer now has ordinary links to courses and programming topic tests. After domain launch, the owner can review links in the RayTech YouTube channel description and relevant lesson descriptions, directing each video to its matching lesson rather than sending every visitor to the homepage. No YouTube account changes are authorized by this document.

## Relevant outreach

Prepare a small list of actual computer-science teachers, college resource librarians, D.I.T course coordinators and educational resource curators. Verify each institution's official contact details before considering contact. Select a resource that serves its readers and explain its exact class, subject and topic. Ask for editorial consideration, not a guaranteed link or a reciprocal placement.

Sending any message requires the owner's separate explicit instruction. Do not create forum accounts, post in groups or edit other websites automatically.

## Link quality

Use descriptive anchors such as D.I.T Computer Networks Chapter 1 practice questions. Keep the anchor natural to the referring page. Avoid purchased ranking links, bulk directory submissions, automated forum comments, private link networks, invented partnerships and keyword-heavy repeated anchors. Sponsored placements, if ever approved, must be disclosed and appropriately qualified; this plan does not authorize any spending.

## Tracking

Record the referring page, target resource URL, editorial relevance, date checked and whether the link still works. Use Search Console's links and performance reports after verification. Record only links that actually exist. Keep removed or broken links in a follow-up list, and preserve useful published slugs. Rankings and indexing are outcomes to observe, not promises.

Reference: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam
