## Offside position and an offside offence are not identical

The basic offside question involves the player's position at the relevant moment and whether that player becomes involved in active play in a way covered by the Law. A player being beyond defenders in a freeze-frame is not, by itself, a complete explanation of every decision.

Timing matters. The relevant moment generally concerns when the ball is played or touched by a teammate, not simply where the attacker stands when the ball eventually arrives. A runner can move into a different position during that interval, which is why pausing at the wrong frame can produce a misleading argument.
