## What to notice when watching a performance

Start with what the character wants in the scene. That immediate objective gives dialogue a purpose. A request for forgiveness, an attempt to hide embarrassment and an effort to persuade a relative can use similar words while requiring different emphasis.

Then notice listening. Acting includes reactions while another character is speaking, not only the delivery of a line. A pause, a change in attention or a restrained response can carry information that a short dialogue compilation removes.

Finally, connect the scene with the wider episode. An emotional response may seem abrupt in isolation while making sense after an earlier event. This is a more useful approach to assessing any actor, including Merub, than assuming that one screenshot or one unusually intense moment can represent an entire serial's work.
