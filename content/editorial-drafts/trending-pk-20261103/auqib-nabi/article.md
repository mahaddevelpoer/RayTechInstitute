## Squad, debut and appearance are three different terms

A squad is an announced group. A debut is the first appearance in a particular recognised category. An appearance is participation in an actual match. A first squad selection does not automatically establish the date or even the occurrence of a debut.

The category matters too. A first-class debut, an India A appearance and a senior ODI debut are different milestones. A profile can accurately discuss one without silently relabelling it as another to make the headline sound larger.

When checking a claim, ask which team and format the writer means. “Made his debut” without those details is incomplete. The October update confirms squad inclusion; this article does not infer a new international cap from that administrative event alone.
