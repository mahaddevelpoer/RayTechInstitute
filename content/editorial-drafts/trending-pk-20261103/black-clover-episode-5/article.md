## Broadcast time and Pakistan time are different questions

The announced Japanese slot was 11:00 p.m. Japan Standard Time. Japan is four hours ahead of Pakistan, so that Japanese broadcast slot corresponds to 7:00 p.m. in Pakistan on the same date. This is a time-zone conversion of the broadcast slot, not a claim about the exact moment every streaming account received the episode.

Crunchyroll's autumn lineup separately lists Black Clover Season 2 with a Saturday 8:00 a.m. Pacific subtitle slot. For 31 October, Pacific daylight time converts that listing to 8:00 p.m. Pakistan time. The difference illustrates why a Japanese television slot and an international platform's release slot should not be merged.

Daylight-saving changes add another reason to check the date. Pakistan keeps UTC+5, but a location described as Pacific Time can change its offset during the year. Reusing a conversion from an earlier Saturday without checking the relevant date can produce an hour's error even when the advertised local time looks unchanged.
