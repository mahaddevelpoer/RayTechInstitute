## Why an unbeaten score matters to the record

The asterisk beside a batting score indicates not out in that innings. It is not a decorative mark or a claim that the match was unfinished. The batter's dismissal status and the match's final status are separate fields.

Not-out innings matter when a batting average is calculated across a sample, because the denominator uses dismissals. Runs divided by appearances is not the same statistic. A short unbeaten contribution can therefore affect an average differently from a dismissed innings with the same score.

That does not mean an average alone captures the complete value of a lower-order role. Time available, wickets remaining and the innings situation provide additional context. The numerical definition should be clear before broader sporting judgments are made.
