## What a dash in a statistics table means

On the reviewed Gujarat Titans listing, Gurnoor's small statistical cells show dashes. The safe reading is that those cells do not supply figures in that display. They are not a declaration that he has never played cricket, taken a wicket or scored a run anywhere.

A dash might reflect an unavailable value, an inactive filter or a category that is not populated. Without a definition from the provider, the exact reason remains unknown. Replacing it with zero changes an absence of displayed information into a numerical claim. That can distort averages, comparisons and a player's basic biography.

If you need his appearances for a particular competition, open that competition's player record and inspect the selected season. For all-format totals, find separate first-class, List A and T20 records. Do not add an uncertain blank to a confirmed number and present the result as an authoritative career total.

## Bowling style: what the label tells you

Right-arm fast-medium identifies the arm used and a broad bowling category. It does not provide the speed of a specific delivery. A claim about the fastest ball needs a measurement linked to a match, not an estimate based on a player's height, run-up or reputation.

Likewise, left-handed batting describes stance rather than ranking a player's batting ability. A bowler can bat left-handed and deliver right-handed without any inconsistency. Batting hand and bowling arm are separate fields on a player profile. Keeping those labels separate prevents a surprisingly common mistake in quick summaries.

To understand an actual spell, watch what happens over several deliveries. Length, direction, movement, the field and the batter's response provide more information than one dramatic wicket clip. Video is still limited evidence: a television angle alone cannot reliably measure release speed or every small movement of the ball.

## Reading a future scorecard correctly

Suppose an imaginary bowler finishes with two wickets for 36 runs in six overs. The economy rate is six runs per over. That is a teaching example, not a Gurnoor performance. The wicket count and runs conceded describe one spell; they do not automatically tell us whether it was the best spell of the match.

If the overs column instead reads 6.4, it normally means six completed overs and four legal balls, or 40 legal deliveries in six-ball cricket. It does not mean 6.4 decimal overs. For an economy calculation, convert those balls to 40 divided by six overs before dividing the conceded runs.

The context also matters. Defending a small target, bowling with a wet ball and facing a late-innings attack are different situations. These are factors to investigate in a match report, not explanations to assume automatically whenever figures look expensive. A scorecard gives outcomes; a fuller account can explain the conditions around them.

## Squad selection is not the same as a debut

An international debut is an appearance in a recognised international match. A squad announcement alone is not enough to date it. Nor should an old auction document's uncapped category be carried forward indefinitely. That label describes the eligibility information used for that historical event.

If someone asks whether Gurnoor has made an ODI debut, use a current official profile or the relevant completed international scorecard. This article does not manufacture a debut date from an unsourced search snippet. The present squad news can be reported accurately while leaving a separate record question open for stronger evidence.

This distinction also applies to tournament medals and final appearances. Being part of a winning franchise group is not identical to playing in its final. Both can be valid achievements, but they need different wording. Precision helps supporters understand the actual contribution rather than reducing every connection to the same generic claim.

## How to follow his progress through the tour

Start with the official fixture identity: opposition, format, venue and date. Next confirm the playing eleven. Then read the bowling figures and, when available, the delivery sequence. This gives a much stronger understanding than following a social post that combines a training photograph with an unrelated score.

Look for repeated opportunities rather than treating one over as a complete evaluation. A bowler may be asked to attack with the new ball, hold a middle-innings spell or manage an expensive final phase. The role should be established from the match, not assigned in advance because another player with a similar style has that job.

For comparisons, choose the same competition and time range. An ODI economy rate and a domestic T20 economy rate come from different playing conditions and incentives. They can inform a broader discussion, but presenting them side by side as an uncomplicated ranking hides the difference in format.
