## What does 49.2 overs mean?

It means forty-nine complete overs and two additional legal balls. In six-ball cricket that is 296 legal balls. It does not mean 49.2 decimal overs, because the digit after the point counts balls rather than tenths of an over.

This distinction affects rate calculations. Oman's 182 runs divided by 296 legal balls and multiplied by six gives approximately 3.69 runs per over. Dividing by the decimal number 49.2 uses a different denominator and produces a subtly incorrect result.

The notation also explains why an innings can finish before the fifty-over allocation is used. Once the batting innings has ended, the unused legal deliveries do not become extra runs. A scorecard records both the total and the opportunity used to reach it.
