### A wicket margin answers a different question from a run margin

## What does 37.3 overs mean?

It means 37 completed six-ball overs plus three legal balls. The total is 37 multiplied by six plus three, or 225 legal balls. It is not a decimal number of overs. In decimal arithmetic, those 225 balls correspond to 37.5 overs. The written cricket notation must be converted before calculating rates or unused deliveries.

### How many legal balls remained?

### Why balls faced and legal balls are not interchangeable

A batter's balls-faced record describes their opportunity count under scoring conventions. A team's over count tracks legal deliveries. Certain extras and scoring decisions mean the two concepts should not be assumed identical when validating a detailed scorecard. Keep separate fields for innings legal balls and each batter's recorded balls faced. An apparent difference deserves checking before editing an official entry.

### Wickets help explain tactical options
