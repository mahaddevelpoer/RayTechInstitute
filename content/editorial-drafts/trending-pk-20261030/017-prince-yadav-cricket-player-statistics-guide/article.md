Prince Yadav is a name readers may encounter in Indian cricket coverage. A useful player guide begins with identity and competition, not an unsupported claim about the latest performance. ## Confirm the player record

Match the full name with the team, season and player profile. Initials and shortened names can hide an important distinction. A franchise squad list records membership at a particular point; it does not prove that the player appeared in every match or remains with the same team in a later season.

This draft does not assert a current contract, transfer, injury or selection. Those details need a dated official announcement. A circulating highlight clip may be authentic while still being attached to the wrong fixture description.

## Understand bowling figures

A spell is usually summarised by overs, maidens, runs conceded and wickets. Economy rate divides conceded runs by overs, with the ball notation handled correctly. For example, 3.4 overs means three overs and four legal balls, not 3.4 decimal overs.

Wickets should be read alongside the match situation. Bowling during the powerplay differs from defending the final overs. Fielding restrictions, batter intent and the required run rate influence the risks on both sides. Compare similar phases before judging improvement or inconsistency.

## Domestic and international records

First-class, List A, domestic T20 and T20I are separate statistical categories. A strong domestic season can be important without already being an international achievement. Do not label franchise wickets as national-team wickets or combine formats into a total without explaining the method.

## A practical scouting note

Select one completed match and record the bowler's spells, lengths attempted, boundary balls and dismissals. Add a sentence on sample size: one good or poor outing cannot establish a complete career pattern. Distinguish observable evidence from a prediction about future selection.
