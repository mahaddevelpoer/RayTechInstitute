Business Recorder reported on 29 October 2026 that PSO's board approved renewal of a sale-purchase agreement with Attock Refinery. A supply agreement concerns commercial arrangements between organisations. It should not be read as an automatic announcement of lower petrol prices or a guaranteed return for shareholders.

## What the report establishes

The report describes a five-year period from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2030, with revised supply commitments and controls around product quality and measurement. These details are attributed to that report. Readers should compare them with the relevant company disclosure before relying on contractual wording.

The start date of an agreement and the date of a board announcement can differ. A later approval report does not mean every commercial activity began on the reporting day. Check whether a document concerns approval, signing, renewal or an amendment.

## Why measurement clauses matter

Petroleum contracts need clear definitions of quantity, quality and delivery. Temperature can affect volume measurement, while sampling and testing help establish whether a product meets the agreed specification. The practical question is how the contract determines what was delivered and whether it is acceptable.

Payment terms, scheduling and dispute procedures can also matter. A short news story may not reproduce those provisions, so their absence from an article is not evidence that the contract contains none.

## Do not confuse the companies

Attock Refinery and other businesses carrying the Attock name are separate legal entities. Likewise, a refinery's processing role differs from an oil-marketing company's distribution role. Match the exact company name and exchange symbol before reading financial statements or announcements.

## What this means for ordinary readers

A renewed supply relationship may be relevant to procurement and operational planning, but its effect on earnings depends on volumes, margins, costs and execution. This page makes no share-price prediction or investment recommendation. For a current pump price, use an effective government notice rather than the contract headline.
