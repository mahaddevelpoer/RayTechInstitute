Kusal Mendis is associated with Sri Lanka's international cricket, but the role in a particular match should be checked on the team sheet. Batting position and wicketkeeping responsibility can change the workload. A useful analysis explains those duties rather than treating every appearance as an identical assignment.

## Begin with the format and position

An ODI innings offers a different scoring horizon from a T20I, while a Test can require a much longer response to changing conditions. Record when the batter arrived, the team score and the immediate task. An innings played after early wickets needs different choices from one beginning with a strong platform.

Runs and strike rate are useful, but they should be connected to the target, remaining overs and available partners. A short sequence of boundaries cannot explain the whole innings.

## Keeping changes what to observe

When the player is the designated keeper, watch starting position, footwork and collection. Catches and stumpings are visible outcomes, but clean takes and communication also help the fielding side. A missed chance needs the delivery and difficulty described before it becomes evidence of a broader weakness.

Do not assume wicketkeeping from an old profile if another player holds the gloves in the current match. The completed scorecard identifies the specific arrangement.

## Selection and leadership claims need dates

A previous captaincy or squad announcement should not be treated as a permanent current title. Use Sri Lanka Cricket's dated statements for selection, injury and role changes. This draft does not invent a live average, ranking or new contract.

## Write a balanced match note

Use one paragraph for the batting task and one for the fielding role. Identify a contribution with evidence and a limitation in the available record. Compare like formats and a defined period before drawing a career conclusion.
