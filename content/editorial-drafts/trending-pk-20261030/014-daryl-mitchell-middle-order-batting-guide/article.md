## Partnerships explain more than an individual total

A partnership records runs while two batters are together, including extras credited to the team. Observe strike rotation, boundary opportunities and which bowler each batter faces. A player can support a faster-scoring partner without dominating every over.

## Interpret the dismissal fairly
