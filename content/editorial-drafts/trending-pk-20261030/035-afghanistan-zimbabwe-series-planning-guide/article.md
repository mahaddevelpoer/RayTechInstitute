An Afghanistan vs Zimbabwe series is a sequence of fixtures, not one scorecard. The Afghanistan Cricket Board's match page is a starting point for the current schedule. To understand the tour, separate the announced programme, individual match results and the final series outcome.

## Make a series timeline

List each match with its number, format, venue and scheduled date. Identify rest days and any change of ground. A tour can be hosted outside either team's home country, so do not infer the location from the order of the team names.

Save the source date beside the schedule. An initial announcement and a later fixture update can differ. If a match is postponed, record the new status rather than leaving two dates that appear to describe separate games.

## Squads are not fixed playing teams

A squad announcement names the available group for that programme. The playing eleven is selected for an individual match. Injuries, workload and tactical choices may change the side, but each explanation needs a reliable announcement rather than speculation from an absence.

Distinguish an official replacement from a predicted line-up. A player training with a squad is not, by itself, proof of selection for the next game.

## Understand the series score

Add only completed results under the competition's rules. A lead before the last fixture may or may not make the series mathematically secure. Ties and no-results need their own labels. A player milestone and a team victory are separate achievements even when they occur in the same match.

## Follow without spreading an old result

Share the match date and source link with any score update. This article does not present a live series table or guarantee the next team selection. Verify changing details at the linked boards before publication or travel planning.
