India is a South Asian country, but a search for its name can refer to geography, government, sport, culture or current affairs. A useful country profile defines its scope before collecting facts. For students, a map and a dated source list are more reliable than a collection of unsourced superlatives.

## Start with location and scale

Locate India within South Asia and identify surrounding countries, seas and major physical regions on an atlas. Distinguish a national border from an internal administrative boundary. Map legends and publication dates matter, especially where a boundary is disputed or a label has changed.

New Delhi is the capital, while other cities serve different commercial, cultural and administrative roles. A capital is not automatically the largest city, and city population depends on whether a source measures municipal limits or a wider urban area.

## Describe diversity accurately

Languages, religions and regional traditions cannot be reduced to one national stereotype. Use official sources for legal language status and a dated census or statistical publication for population claims. A widely spoken language and a constitutionally specified category are not identical concepts.

Avoid presenting a single festival, meal or film industry as the experience of every resident. Explain the region or community relevant to an example.

## Economic facts need dates

GDP, growth and income per person measure different things. A ranking can depend on nominal values, purchasing-power comparisons and the reporting period. State the definition instead of repeating a claim that a country is simply the biggest or fastest without context.

## Keep sport in its own category

India's cricket team is not the same subject as the country as a whole. For a fixture, confirm the format and opponent on the cricket board's website. For a school country profile, focus on geography, institutions and sourced statistics.

Create a one-page summary with a map, five facts and a source beside each. Our [country terminology guide](/blogs/what-is-a-country-state-geography-explained/) explains why membership lists and political geography require careful definitions.
