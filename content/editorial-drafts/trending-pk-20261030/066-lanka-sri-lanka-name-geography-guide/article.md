Lanka can appear as part of Sri Lanka's name or as a shortened reference in conversation, sport and search results. The intended meaning depends on context. For a modern country profile, use Sri Lanka and identify whether you are discussing geography, travel, language or its national cricket team.

## Locate the country before collecting facts

Sri Lanka is an island country in the Indian Ocean, near the southern part of India. A map helps distinguish the island from the wider South Asian region. Physical location and political identity are related but should not be treated as the same concept.

For administrative details and statistics, use an official or institutional source with a clear date. A tourism page can introduce places and culture, but it is not automatically the best source for every population or legal figure.

## Shortened names can be ambiguous

A search for Lanka may return cricket scores, airline information, historical references or cultural material. Add a second term such as geography, tourism or fixtures to make the intention clear. Do not assume the first result answers every use of the word.

Historical and literary references also require their own sources. A name in a story is not enough to establish a modern political boundary or an archaeological claim.

## Travel information changes

Entry conditions, transport arrangements and site opening times should be checked through current official channels. A country introduction is not a visa guarantee. Nationality, passport validity and purpose of travel can affect the applicable requirements.

For cultural study, avoid presenting one food, festival or language as the experience of every resident. Explain the region and community relevant to the example.

## A simple research task

Write a short profile with a map, three sourced facts and one paragraph explaining the context in which you encountered the name. Keep a cricket-team result separate from a national statistic.
