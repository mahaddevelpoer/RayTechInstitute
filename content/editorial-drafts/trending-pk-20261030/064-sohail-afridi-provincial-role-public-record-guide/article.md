Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Assembly's profile identifies Muhammad Sohail Afridi and describes his public role. The official member and portfolio pages are useful starting points for civic research. They should be read with their dates and institutional context rather than used to support every claim circulating in political commentary.

## Identify the institution

A provincial assembly, a provincial government and a political party perform different functions. Membership of one body does not by itself explain authority over every public service. For a particular decision, identify the responsible department and the document that records the action.

Constituency information belongs to a defined electoral term. Archived profiles can remain online after a role changes, so compare the page with the current members and portfolios listings.

## Announcement, approval and delivery differ

A speech may announce an intention. Administrative approval, a budget allocation, procurement and actual delivery can follow as separate stages. A project should not be described as completed simply because it was mentioned at an event.

For an education initiative, useful questions include eligibility, implementing department, application method and the official effective date. Do not invent those details from a broad promise or a promotional graphic.

## Read political clips in context

Find the full statement where possible. A short clip can omit the question, qualification or date. Separate a verified quotation from an interpretation of its political significance, and avoid unsupported allegations about motives or conduct.

This article does not endorse a party or predict an election outcome. It explains how to check a public record and follow the process behind a headline.

## Build a small evidence table

Use columns for claim, responsible institution, dated source and implementation status. Mark an item unconfirmed when the relevant document is missing. That is more useful than repeating a claim because several social accounts share it.
