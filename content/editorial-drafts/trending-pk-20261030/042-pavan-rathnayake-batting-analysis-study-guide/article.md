## Find the match behind the clip

Check the video description, upload date and competition. Upload time is not always the date the match was played. A highlighted innings from a development side or domestic tournament should not automatically be labelled a senior international performance.

This guide does not claim a current selection or new career record. Use a dated board announcement for those changing details and a completed scorecard for the numbers.

## Break the innings into phases

Start with the first few deliveries: was the batter assessing movement, rotating strike or attacking immediately? Then identify when the scoring pattern changed. A boundary sequence may follow a change of bowler, field or required run rate.

Runs and balls faced describe output and pace. They do not explain every choice. Note which scoring areas were available and whether the batter kept finding singles between boundaries. Dot balls can reveal pressure that a highlights package leaves out.

## Examine risk without hindsight

A successful attacking shot still carries risk, and a dismissal does not automatically make the decision unreasonable. Judge the option using the match situation and delivery rather than only the outcome. A shot needed to chase a high target can be unnecessary in a different context.

Avoid announcing a permanent technical strength from one clip. A larger sample against different bowlers and conditions is needed for that conclusion.

Create three short paragraphs: entry situation, scoring development and dismissal or finish. Support each with a specific scorecard entry or observed delivery.
