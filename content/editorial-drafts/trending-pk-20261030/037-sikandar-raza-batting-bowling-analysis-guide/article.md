## What was the batting task?

Record the entry score, target and available overs. Rebuilding after wickets requires different choices from finishing an innings with partners available. Strike rate helps describe pace, while boundary choices and strike rotation explain how the runs were made.

A short innings can produce an extreme strike rate. Use a defined sample of matches before claiming a lasting pattern. Keep domestic league figures separate from T20I or ODI records.

## What was the bowling task?

A supporting spell may aim to control scoring or exploit a particular matchup. Wickets matter, but economy, dot balls and the timing of the spell add context. A bowler facing an aggressive late-innings pair is working under different conditions from one bowling after early wickets.

Do not turn a change in overs bowled into an injury claim without a team announcement. Match tactics and available options can explain a different allocation.
