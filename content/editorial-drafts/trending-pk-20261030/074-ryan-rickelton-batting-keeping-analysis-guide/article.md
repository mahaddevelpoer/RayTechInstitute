## Starting an innings has several tasks

When opening, a batter faces the new ball and helps establish the team's position. Movement, bounce and field restrictions influence available options. A quiet first over may be deliberate assessment, while an early boundary does not establish that every attacking shot is low risk.

Record runs, balls and the team's situation. Compare innings within the same format because a Test opening spell and a T20 powerplay involve different scoring horizons.

## Watch the response to bowling changes

A new bowler can change angle, pace or length. Observe whether the batter adjusts footwork and scoring areas. Strike rotation can keep an innings moving even when boundaries become harder to find. A highlights package may omit those singles and the deliveries that created pressure.

Do not infer a permanent technical weakness from one dismissal. A meaningful claim needs a pattern across a defined sample and the relevant conditions.
