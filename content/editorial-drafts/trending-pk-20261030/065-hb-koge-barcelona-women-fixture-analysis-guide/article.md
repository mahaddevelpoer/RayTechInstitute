## Get the fixture identity right

## Read the game beyond the reputation

A club's history does not determine every minute of a match. Watch how each side builds possession, protects the centre and responds to pressure. A team facing a stronger opponent may choose a compact shape and look for transitions rather than competing for possession in the same way.

Shots, chances and field position add context, but no single statistic describes every tactical choice. A large scoreline still needs the sequence of goals and match conditions explained accurately.
