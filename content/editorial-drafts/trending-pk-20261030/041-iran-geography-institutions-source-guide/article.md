Iran is a West Asian country and a neighbour of Pakistan. A useful introduction separates physical geography, population, institutions and current events. These subjects are connected, but a breaking-news headline should not become the sole description of a country or the people who live there.

## Begin with a map and clear names

Locate Iran relative to Pakistan, the Persian Gulf and the Caspian Sea. Distinguish Tehran, the capital, from the wider country. Political boundaries and physical features need different symbols, so read the map legend rather than treating every line as the same kind of border.

The UN uses the formal name Iran (Islamic Republic of) in its country records. A formal institutional label and the shorter everyday name can refer to the same state. Keep the source's terminology when quoting a statistical table.

## Statistics need a year

Population, national income and trade values change over time. UN country data can bring several indicators together, but the observations may come from different years. Read the footnotes instead of assuming everything on a single page describes the same month.

A national average does not describe each region, household or community. Language, culture and daily life are more varied than a short profile can show.

## Treat current affairs as dated reporting

For an announcement, identify the institution making it and the date. Distinguish a proposal from an implemented measure and a claim from independently documented evidence. A video of an event needs a verified location and time before it can support a current headline.

This article does not claim a live security development, visa condition or border status. Those details require current official information and, where appropriate, reliable independent reporting.

## A country-study exercise

Make four sections: geography, society, economy and institutions. Add two sourced facts to each and record the year where it matters. Then write one question that your sources do not answer. This teaches careful research rather than collecting dramatic claims. Our [country terminology guide](/blogs/what-is-a-country-state-geography-explained/) explains related concepts.
