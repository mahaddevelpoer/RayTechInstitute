AFG vs ZIM is a common abbreviation for Afghanistan against Zimbabwe. To follow the right game, confirm the date, venue and format on the Afghanistan Cricket Board's match page. An abbreviated search term cannot establish whether a result is live, scheduled or already completed.

## Before the first ball

Check the match number and the competition. A bilateral T20I series is different from an ODI, a Test or a short-format exhibition. The announced squad is not the same as the confirmed playing eleven. Wait for the team sheet before treating a predicted selection as a fact.

Convert the start time using the venue's stated time zone. A fixture hosted outside either team's home country can make assumptions about local time unreliable. Weather and earlier scheduling changes should be checked through an official update.

## During the innings

Read runs, wickets and overs together. In a chase, add the target and required rate. A quick fifty can be important, but its meaning changes depending on whether the team is ahead of or behind the required scoring pace.

Track extras and bowling figures as well as boundaries. Pressure can build through dot balls or a tight spell even when the highlights show only wickets and sixes. Record the over and score beside any tactical observation.

## At the end of the match

Use the full-time result and completed scorecard. A late wicket, interruption or corrected scoring entry can make an earlier screenshot incomplete. Separate the player-of-the-match award from a personal opinion about the most impressive performer.

For record claims, identify the statistical category. International wickets, domestic wickets and all-format totals should not be silently combined. Our [Rashid Khan milestone guide](/blogs/rashid-khan-200-t20i-wickets-record-explained/) explains that distinction.
