## How do highlights differ from a complete account?

Highlights select moments from a match. They can show boundaries, wickets and celebrations while omitting much of the play between them. That makes them entertaining and informative, but not a complete sample for every statistical or tactical conclusion. A result and a full scorecard provide other kinds of information.

Suppose an invented highlights clip contains five sixes and no dot balls. You cannot conclude that the innings contained no dots. The selection rule favoured exciting moments. This is a simple example of sample bias: what appears in the clip is not necessarily distributed like everything that happened in the match.
