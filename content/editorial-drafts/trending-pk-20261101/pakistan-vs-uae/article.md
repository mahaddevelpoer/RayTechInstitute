## How can a required rate help a beginner?

A required rate expresses a remaining run requirement over the remaining allocation. It is a descriptive average, not a command to score an exact amount in every over. In a fictional case needing eighteen from six legal balls, the average requirement is eighteen per six-ball over, or three per ball.

Scoring does not arrive evenly. A dot ball increases the rate needed from what remains; a boundary can reduce it. The underlying state changes after each event. That makes a chase a useful example of a small dynamic system whose next requirement depends on both the previous state and the new action.
