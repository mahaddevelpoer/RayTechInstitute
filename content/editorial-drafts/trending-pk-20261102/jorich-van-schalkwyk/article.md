## Why distinguish youth cricket from senior internationals?

Youth competitions have their own teams, eligibility rules and statistical categories. A Youth ODI is not automatically a senior ODI, even when both are fifty-over matches. The word ODI can therefore be misleading if its youth qualifier is removed from a profile or headline.

## How is batting average calculated?

Batting average divides runs by dismissals in the defined sample. It is not usually runs divided by innings when some innings are not out. The denominator is part of the measure's definition. A high average from a small number of dismissals also needs its sample size shown clearly.

Use an original fictional sequence of 30, 40 not out and 50. The total is 120 runs with two dismissals, giving an average of sixty. Dividing by three innings would give forty, which is a different calculation and should not be labelled the conventional batting average.

If no dismissal has occurred in the sample, the ordinary runs-per-dismissal ratio has no finite denominator. Do not display a zero average. State the limitation or use the convention specified by the record provider. An honest undefined field is more informative than a convenient but incorrect number.
