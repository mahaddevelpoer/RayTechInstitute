## What changes between twenty-over and fifty-over cricket?

The available innings length changes how a side can distribute risk and resources. A twenty-over innings has fewer legal deliveries than a fifty-over innings under the standard six-ball representation. That arithmetic affects the time available to recover from a slow period, but it does not prescribe one correct tactic for every match.
