## Why does added time differ from extra time?

Added time extends a period to account for time lost under the match officials' application of the rules. Extra time is a separate procedure used in some competitions when required to decide a match. A routine league goal at 45+2 is not automatically an extra-time goal.

## What is the difference between a lead and a win?

A lead describes an intermediate state; a win describes the completed outcome. At 1–0, a side is ahead but has not necessarily won. A later equaliser or reversal remains possible until the match's final status is established. A live component should preserve that distinction rather than publishing a final verdict too early.
