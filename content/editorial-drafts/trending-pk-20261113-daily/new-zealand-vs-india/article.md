## Convert 9.4 overs before calculating a rate

Henry's 9.4 overs means nine completed overs and four further legal balls. Nine multiplied by six plus four equals 58 legal balls. It does not mean 9.4 decimal overs, because the digit after the dot is counting balls within the incomplete over.

Using the recorded return, his economy is 27 multiplied by six and divided by 58, approximately 2.79 runs per over. His bowling average for this innings is 27 divided by six, or 4.5 runs per wicket. His bowling strike rate is 58 divided by six, approximately 9.67 legal balls per wicket.

These calculations have different units. Economy measures runs per over, average measures runs per wicket, and bowling strike rate measures balls per wicket. A single number without its unit cannot communicate which question is being answered. Write the label beside the value when building a graphic or study note.
