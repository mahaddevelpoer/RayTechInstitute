## What all-rounder means

The term indicates potential contribution in more than one main discipline, usually batting and bowling. It does not mean that the player performs the same role in every team or produces equal returns in both areas every time.

One side may need more overs from a player, while another may use the same player at a different batting position. To assess the contribution, first identify the actual opportunity and task. A title on a profile cannot supply that context by itself.

For a learning note, create separate sections for batting and bowling before trying to combine them into an overall view. That avoids an easy mistake: adding unrelated units as though thirty runs and two wickets form one simple mathematical score.
