## Points and goal difference answer different questions

Points record the competition return from wins, draws and losses. Goal difference is goals scored minus goals conceded across the relevant set of matches. Both can appear in a table, but they are not interchangeable measurements and should not be combined into an invented score.

For an illustrative example, a team scoring eight and conceding five has a goal difference of plus three. Another team scoring ten and conceding seven also has plus three. The equal difference does not mean their individual match results or points must be the same.
