## What is the HEC China scholarship update?

HEC currently lists the Chinese Government Scholarship Programme for 2027-28 with 61 places across undergraduate, master's and doctoral study. Pakistani applicants should check their eligibility, arrange the required test evidence and complete the relevant online applications. HEC's announced portal deadline is **5 January 2027 at 4:00 PM Pakistan time**.

**Source check: 28 November 2026.** This guide reports the current HEC pages; it does not claim that the programme was announced today. If an official instruction changes, follow the updated HEC notice.

## Which study levels and applicants are covered?

The seat distribution and the education/age conditions below come from HEC's announcement and eligibility page. An age limit alone does not establish eligibility: academic completion, nationality, language and university requirements also matter.

| Study level | Announced places | Education and age conditions stated by HEC |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Bachelor's | 25 | Intermediate/A-level/FA/FSc or equivalent 12-year education; under 25 when applying |
| Master's | 21 | A 16-year bachelor's qualification; under 35 when applying |
| PhD | 15 | An MS/MPhil-level master's qualification; under 40 when applying |

HEC identifies Pakistani/AJ&K nationals as the applicant group and excludes dual-nationality holders. Its eligibility page also excludes applicants whose required degree is incomplete at the closing date. Students still taking school or college examinations should therefore compare their actual qualification status with the programme rules, rather than assuming that the age condition is sufficient.

Use the [official eligibility checklist](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/lao/CGSP/Pages/Eligibility-Criteria.aspx) for the full conditions, including requirements set by the intended university.

## Which applications must be submitted?

Use both application systems; an HEC form alone does not complete the CSC application.

1. Complete your profile and the relevant Learning Opportunities Abroad application at the [HEC scholarship portal](https://scholarship.hec.gov.pk/).
2. Follow HEC's guide for the CSC form at [Campus China](https://www.campuschina.org/). For its nomination route, HEC lists **Type A** and **agency number 5861**.
3. Submit both forms, check the status and keep the acknowledgements. A saved draft is not a completed submission.
4. Follow stage-specific document instructions. HEC's guide says not to post hard copies to HEC or the Chinese Embassy at this stage.

Use the [official application guide](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/lao/CGSP/Pages/How-to-Apply.aspx) to check the portal steps if interface labels change. This article's dated deadline refers to HEC's portal; read CSC and university instructions for their own submission steps and timing.

## What should students check about USAT, HAT and CSCA?

HEC specifies **USAT for undergraduate applicants** and **HAT for master's/PhD applicants**, with a minimum stated score of **50 out of 100**. It also specifies accepted result windows. Having a score report is therefore different from having a report that qualifies for this particular scholarship cycle.

Undergraduate applicants must also check the **CSCA** requirement and ask their chosen university which subjects apply. It does not replace the USAT requirement.

The programme pages still show November test-registration wording. Checked on 28 November 2026, this should not be assumed to remain open. Consult the latest [ETC announcements](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Pages/Announcements.aspx). Test registration and scholarship submission have separate deadlines.

## Which documents should applicants prepare?

HEC's [required-documents page](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/lao/CGSP/Pages/Required-Documents.aspx) describes a later attested/translated checklist for shortlisted or nominated candidates. Follow the upload requirements shown for your own application stage; do not assume every later-stage attestation must be completed before you can read the initial application instructions.

Its checklist includes academic and identity records, the study plan/research proposal, applicable language evidence and passport documentation. It states a study-plan minimum of 200 words for undergraduate applicants and 800 words for master's/PhD applicants. Recommendation letters are listed for master's/PhD candidates; employer or guardian documents depend on the applicant's circumstances.

A practical way to organise your work is to keep a simple application tracker:

| Item to track | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | The rule you checked and the qualification evidence you hold |
| Test evidence | Test name, result date, score and the accepted-window instruction |
| Documents | File name, current version and anything still missing |
| HEC application | Portal status and acknowledgement |
| CSC application | Portal status and acknowledgement |
| Follow-up | The official message, its date and the action requested |

This tracker is a study/planning aid, not an additional HEC requirement. Use clear file names, compare names and dates across documents, and keep a copy of the instructions you relied on. Ask the official programme contact about an unclear condition instead of guessing an answer on the form.

## What should I do before the deadline?

Begin with the eligibility and test checks, because they determine whether collecting the remaining material is useful for this cycle. Then prepare the requested evidence and complete each portal's submission steps. Leave time to correct unreadable scans, mismatched details or an unfinished form.

Before closing the application, check the official announcement again and verify that the portal status shows a completed submission. HEC nomination is a selection stage; submitting a form is not a scholarship award.
