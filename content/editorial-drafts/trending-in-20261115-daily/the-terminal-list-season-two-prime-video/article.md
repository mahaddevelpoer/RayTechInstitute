Amazon's [official season guide](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/entertainment/terminal-list-prime-video-chris-pratt), updated on 21 October 2026, confirms the availability, episode count, principal cast and basic plot setup. ### What is Season 2 about, without major spoilers?

The official description says Reece is looking for a new purpose after completing the list that drove the first season. The story draws from *True Believer* and broadens into a conspiracy with international reach. Amazon describes a path across the Indian Ocean, Africa, the Middle East and Europe, with intrigue stretching between Moscow and Langley. Those are promotional plot settings, not proof that each episode was filmed in every named place. We can describe the story's announced scope without inventing scene-by-scene locations or hidden twists.

This shift is the main difference in premise. Season 1 centered on a psychological revenge drive. Season 2 asks what a character shaped by that experience does next and places him in a wider espionage network. That helps viewers decide whether to begin immediately after Season 1. A person who expects a simple repetition of the original list structure may miss the point of the new title. The official description suggests a journey outward, with more international relationships and questions about Reece's past.

The series remains an action thriller. The Amazon guide talks about conspiratorial forces and moral complexity, while the creator announcement names veteran advisers and technical contributors. These descriptions communicate the production's intentions. They are not an independent review or a guarantee that every depicted operation mirrors real-life procedure.
