## Stoppage time is not another period of play

The notation 90+1 identifies an event in added time associated with the second half. It is not a decimal minute and does not mean that a separate extra-time period began. Extra time in a knockout setting is a different concept from time added for interruptions during normal play.

That distinction matters when a goal log uses 91 while another uses 90+1. The entries may be two presentations of the same minute rather than conflicting events.
