## Why does the exact product matter?

A stitched garment, unstitched fabric, fragrance and accessory are different product types. An image containing a complete outfit does not necessarily mean every pictured item is included. ## What should a shopper check about size?

## How can a percentage discount be checked?

The discount percentage compares the reduction with the stated reference price. It is not a measurement of quality, suitability or affordability. A reliable comparison records both the reference and final amounts, along with the exact item and any condition attached to the offer.

Use an invented item reduced from four thousand rupees to three thousand. The reduction is one thousand, and dividing that by four thousand gives twenty-five percent. These are classroom figures, not a current J. offer or a claim that any particular item is available at that price.

Now compare another fictional item reduced from two thousand to one thousand. Its percentage reduction is larger, but its contents and usefulness may be different. A larger discount label alone cannot establish which purchase meets the shopper's needs. Compare the actual product before deciding that a percentage settles the question.

## Is a return the same as a cash refund?

A return process, an exchange and store credit are different outcomes. Do not assume that one word establishes the others. The official policy's refund and voucher sections should be checked for the particular arrangement, rather than describing every approved complaint as money returned to the original payment method.

For a fictional exercise, compare an exchange for another item with a voucher that can be used later. These resolve a problem in different ways and can carry different conditions. A shopper who understands the distinction can ask a clearer question before accepting a proposed resolution.

Keep the seller's written response with the order record. An attempted support message and a completed remedy are not the same event. This guide cannot inspect an individual order or promise what support will decide; it explains which details make the question understandable and verifiable.

## Can sale images and countdowns be misleading?
