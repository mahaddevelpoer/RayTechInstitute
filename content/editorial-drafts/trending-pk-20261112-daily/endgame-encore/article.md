## What is colour grading?

Colour grading is part of the finishing process in which the appearance of images is shaped across a film. It can influence contrast, colour relationships and the mood of a scene. A revised grade can make a familiar sequence look different without changing the dialogue, the actor's performance or the order of events.

Think of a simple classroom example: the same photograph can be presented with warmer or cooler tones. The subject remains recognisable, but the atmosphere changes. Film grading is more involved than applying a casual photo filter, yet the example illustrates why a visual revision and an additional scene are different kinds of change.
