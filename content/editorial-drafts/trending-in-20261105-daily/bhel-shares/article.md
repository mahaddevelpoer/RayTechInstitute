## Order intake and order book differ

Order intake measures new business under a stated definition during a period. The order book describes defined outstanding business at a reference date. Execution, scope changes and other adjustments can connect them, so neither should be assumed to equal the other.

For a fictional opening order book of 500, new orders of 200 and execution of 150, a simplified closing figure is 550 before other adjustments. The 200 added during the year is not the closing 550. Including both as separate future sales would double count part of the picture.

Read the report's actual bridge where available. An order-book figure needs its date and scope, while a new-order figure needs its period. A large balance can indicate substantial work to execute without establishing when every part will become revenue or cash.

## A tender opportunity is not an awarded contract

An invitation to bid, a bid submission, a preferred position and a completed award are different stages. The official announcement should establish which stage occurred. A possible market opportunity should not be reported as secured business just because its potential value is large.

## Questions about BHEL shares

### Is BHEL the same as Bharat Electronics?

No. Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited and Bharat Electronics Limited are different companies. Confirm the full legal name and the identified security before using results or news. Similar abbreviations do not make their orders, financial statements or share prices interchangeable.

### Does a new order immediately become profit?

No. An award, execution, recognised revenue and final profit concern different stages. Costs and accounting treatment matter, and cash collection can follow another timeline. Use the actual project and financial disclosures rather than copying a contract amount into a quarterly profit estimate.

### Is an order book the same as annual revenue?

No. The order book is a defined balance of outstanding business at a date. Revenue concerns recognised activity during a period. Execution and adjustments connect them, but the figures should remain separately labelled and should not be added as unrelated completed sales.
