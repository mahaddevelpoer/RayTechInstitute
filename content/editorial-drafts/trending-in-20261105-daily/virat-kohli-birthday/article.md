## How old is Virat Kohli in November 2026?

Kohli is 38 after his birthday on 5 November 2026. Subtracting 1988 from 2026 gives 38, and his birthday has already occurred by the review date. ## How should batting average and strike rate be read?

Batting average and strike rate describe different relationships. In standard batting statistics, average relates runs to dismissals, while strike rate relates runs to balls faced. Not-outs therefore matter to the average's denominator. Neither measure is simply another spelling of total runs.

Use an invented record of two hundred runs across four dismissals. The average in that model is fifty. If the same two hundred runs came from two hundred and fifty balls, the strike rate is eighty runs per hundred balls. Those calculations describe different quantities and do not evaluate every aspect of the innings.

These are fictional numbers, not Kohli's current statistics. Their purpose is to make the definitions clear before readers compare actual records. A meaningful comparison also considers format, period, innings and match context rather than declaring one player better from an isolated percentage.

## What does captaincy add to a career profile?

Captaincy introduces team responsibilities that differ from individual batting. Selection discussions, field settings, bowling changes and wider team decisions involve several people and constraints. A profile should not claim access to a player's private decision-making unless a reliable public account establishes it.

Use official match records to identify when someone captained and documented interviews to explain public statements. Avoid invented dressing-room scenes or motivational quotations. For students, distinguishing an individual's performance from team leadership is a valuable analytical habit.
