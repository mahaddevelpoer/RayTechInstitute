Thibaut Courtois was UEFA's Player of the Match when Real Madrid defeated Liverpool 1-0 in the 2022 Champions League final. The result is a useful starting point for understanding goalkeeping because a clean sheet, a series of saves and a team victory are connected without being the same statistic. The goalkeeper's work also includes positioning, decisions and communication that a final score alone cannot fully describe.

This guide follows official club, FIFA and UEFA records. It uses the documented final and clearly fictional exercises to explain goalkeeping measures. It does not invent a current injury update, transfer, appearance total or medical assessment from a historical photograph.

## A clean sheet is a team-level outcome

A clean sheet means the team did not concede in the relevant match or defined appearance context. It is important, but it does not independently describe how many attempts the goalkeeper faced or how difficult the work was.

## Shot difficulty is not captured by count alone

Two attempts can differ in distance, angle, speed, obstruction and the surrounding play. A count treats them as separate events, while a fuller analysis needs the situation. A low number of shots does not automatically mean an easy match.

## Positioning shapes the available angle

A goalkeeper's location relative to the ball and goal changes the angle available to an attacker. The useful position depends on the developing situation. A still frame taken before the final pass cannot necessarily establish the best position at the moment of the shot.

## Handling and deflection are different outcomes

A goalkeeper may retain the ball or redirect it away from the immediate attempt. The consequences depend on where the ball goes and what happens next. A stopped first effort is not always the end of the attacking sequence.

In an invented example, a deflection leaves the ball in play and the defence responds to the rebound. The goalkeeper's first action and the next team action should be described separately. Calling the move finished before the rebound would omit relevant context.

## Crosses create a different decision problem

A cross can involve the goalkeeper, defenders, attackers and the ball's path through a shared space. Its interpretation differs from a direct shot. A goalkeeper touching a cross should not automatically be added to a save tally without the relevant statistical definition.

For a fictional observation, note where the ball is delivered, which players are nearby and whether the goalkeeper retains or redirects it. These fields help explain the event without guessing private communication or instructions.

The broader lesson is to preserve event types. A strong goalkeeper profile can discuss several responsibilities while keeping the formal statistics accurate. Combining every defensive action under one dramatic label makes the record less clear rather than more impressive.

## Distribution begins another phase of play

After gaining possession, a goalkeeper can help start the next attack through the available passing options. Completion, distance and purpose are different aspects of that distribution. A long pass and a short pass should not be assumed identical tasks merely because both can be completed.

## Communication is difficult to measure from a silent clip

## Individual awards and team trophies are different

Courtois's Player of the Match honour in the 2022 final is an individual award attached to a particular game. Madrid's Champions League title is a team achievement attached to the competition. The two should retain their labels.

An award from one final does not mean the player received every season-long or calendar-year honour. Categories and dates matter. A photograph holding one trophy should not be captioned with the name of another award merely because both are associated with the player.

## Questions about Thibaut Courtois

### What award did he receive in the 2022 Champions League final?

UEFA named him Player of the Match in Madrid's 1-0 win over Liverpool. The category and event are important. It is an individual honour attached to that final, separate from the team's competition trophy and from awards covering other periods.

### Does a clean sheet reveal the number of saves?

No. It records a no-goals-conceded outcome under the relevant context. The goalkeeper's workload needs the actual attempts and event record. Two clean sheets can involve very different numbers and types of opportunities faced.

### Is every touch by a goalkeeper a save?

No. Crosses, passes and other actions have different classifications. Use the provider's definition for an official save count. A useful description can discuss all these responsibilities without relabelling every touch as the same statistical event.
