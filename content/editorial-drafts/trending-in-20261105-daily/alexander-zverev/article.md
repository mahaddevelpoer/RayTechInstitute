## How should a tennis set score be interpreted?

A set score records games, while a match score lists the completed sets under the applicable format. The numbers therefore have levels. A 6-3 set is not a six-match-to-three-match result, and a four-set final does not mean the winner won four separate tournaments.

Take the documented 2026 US Open final as an example of reading the sequence. The first 6-3 is one set, the 7-6 is another, and the later 5-7 belongs to a set won by Shelton. Keep the order before trying to explain how the overall match was decided.

Rules and formats can differ by competition, so consult the relevant event's regulations for a current question. ## What do the numbers in parentheses mean?
