## Editorial boundaries

RIL differs from Reliance Industrial Infrastructure and Jio Financial. JFS holding, operating entities and client flows are distinct; no current product-launch status assumed from older future-tense text. Adani Power October 2026 filing establishes entity and scope; no dynamic capacity total asserted. GAIL older annual tables are labelled historical; useful-life revision is an accounting item, not a cash inflow. Tata Steel physical quantities, regional reporting and environmental intensity are kept separate. Bajaj Finance FY26 adjusted highlighted measures retain exclusion qualifications; no adjusted profit is mislabelled statutory. MCX shares differ from derivative contracts, notional turnover and client funds. Titan FY26 page has inconsistent jewellery subtotals, so no conflicting exact revenue figure was copied; Damas timing is discussed qualitatively with dated context. Asian Paints decorative, industrial and decor categories follow official report. BHEL April update is explicitly provisional and unaudited; BEL is a separate company. Images require visual inspection before publication; unrelated Essar refinery and misleading GAIL maps were rejected.
