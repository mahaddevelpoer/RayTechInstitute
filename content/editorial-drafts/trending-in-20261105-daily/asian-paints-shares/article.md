## Questions about Asian Paints shares

### Does volume growth always mean revenue growth?

No. Selling value and product mix can change alongside physical quantity. The fictional examples illustrate how the two can move differently. Use the company's actual volume and value measures rather than treating either one as a substitute for the other.

### Does cheaper crude oil guarantee a wider margin?

No. Several inputs, timing effects, selling values and operating costs can affect the result. The company's actual reporting is needed to connect market context with financial performance. One commodity chart does not establish a complete cost or profit forecast.

### Can a retail paint price be used to calculate company revenue?

Not without the relevant commercial and accounting information. A final retail transaction may include components belonging to other participants or services. Use recognised company revenue and disclosed operating measures rather than multiplying an unrelated shop quotation by reported volumes.
