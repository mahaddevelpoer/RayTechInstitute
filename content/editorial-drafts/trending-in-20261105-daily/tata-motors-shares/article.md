## More holdings do not automatically mean more wealth

A restructuring can change the number or type of securities a person holds under its terms. The appearance of an additional line in a portfolio is not, by itself, evidence that wealth has doubled. The values and rights associated with the resulting holdings need to be considered together.

## Unit sales and revenue are different measures

A vehicle-sales count describes units under the company's definition. Revenue depends on the products, prices, timing and accounting scope. A percentage increase in units is not automatically the same percentage increase in revenue, and neither establishes profit on its own.

## Profit, cash flow and investment belong in separate sections

Revenue and profit measure different stages of a financial statement. Operating cash flow adds a different view, while investment spending has its own treatment and timing. A single headline cannot replace all three.
