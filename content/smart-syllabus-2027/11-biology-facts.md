The Biology deletion table is organised by chapter, topic and exercise reference. In Biodiversity and Classification it identifies named material including taxonomic hierarchy, classification of vertebrates and viruses, biodiversity, and species/speciation. It separately identifies MCQs, long questions and all inquisitive questions for that row.

Bacteria and Viruses includes motility, ecology/diversity and importance of bacteria, alongside selected exercise questions. Cells and Subcellular Organelles identifies the specified introductory cell material, cell theory, microscopy, techniques for studying the plasma membrane and the comparison of eukaryotic/prokaryotic cells. The Molecular Biology and Enzymes rows continue with their own selective exclusions.

Some entries concern a figure or only part of a figure: a diagram reference must not be interpreted as deletion of every concept on the page. The remaining chapter entries follow in the PDF. The written examination is 85 marks, with 17 objective and 68 subjective marks, and three hours overall: twenty minutes objective and two hours forty minutes subjective. The nine-page subject file includes the complete table and assessment material.
