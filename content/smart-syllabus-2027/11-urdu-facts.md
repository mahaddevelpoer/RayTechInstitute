The Grade 11 Urdu ALP identifies four excluded selections. Two are prose: Charpai by Rashid Ahmad Siddiqui, pages 44-49, and Pakistan ki Zabanain aur Un ka Bahami Rishta by Dr Masood Husain Khan, pages 90-96. The poem is Dhol Wala by Syed Muhammad Jafri, pages 116-119.

The ghazal entry is the Mir Taqi Mir selection beginning with Patta patta boota boota haal hamara jaane hai, pages 121-125. Identify the original Urdu title and opening line from the document when marking your textbook, since Roman transliteration is not standardised across notes.

The notice directs paper setters not to set objective, subjective or other questions from the excluded selections. It does not remove the remaining prose, poetry or composition requirements. The written paper carries 100 marks, with 20 objective and 80 subjective marks. The ten-page subject PDF includes the detailed assessment material following the exclusion notice.
