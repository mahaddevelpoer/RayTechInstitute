The Physics table reduces selected topics and exercise questions. In Measurements it names physical quantities and their units and the international system of units, alongside separate MCQ, short-answer, constructed-response, comprehensive-question and numerical references. A heading appearing here does not remove every calculation that uses a unit.

Force and Motion includes selected scalar/vector material, the graphical derivation of equations of motion, two-dimensional collision material and rocket propulsion. Circular and Rotational Motion identifies applications of centripetal force, the flywheel and the gyroscope, with individual exercise references. Work, Energy and Power also has partial rather than blanket deletions.

The table continues through the remaining chapters. Read its page and exercise columns alongside the topic column, especially where an entry deletes only part of a comprehensive question. The written paper is 85 marks, with 17 objective and 68 subjective marks. It allows three hours in total: twenty minutes for objective questions and two hours forty minutes for the subjective part.
