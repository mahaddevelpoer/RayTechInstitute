## Syllabus mapping and limits

The mapping follows the user-supplied PBTE first-semester outline. It covers 33 ICT practical bullets, 60 Office practicals, 14 Networking practicals, 25 numbered OS practicals plus four additional tasks, and 29 actually printed Python practicals. The Python outline mentions a larger range elsewhere but its practical list ends at 29; entries 30 and 31 were not invented. ICT workbook task numbers are local navigation numbers for the outline's unnumbered bullets.

The notes explicitly correct the outline's Kotlin/Python classification, `len()` function terminology, legacy classful addressing context, 10BASE-T cabling and unsupported PowerPoint 2016 Zoom creation. Legacy Windows/Office exercises are described as syllabus lab work with current-support context.

These are original study guides, not official PBTE textbooks, authenticated past papers or promises of examination questions. Windows installation, Office desktop UI, physical networking/Packet Tracer and native Turtle GUI tasks are written student lab instructions, not claims of executed hardware/GUI validation.
