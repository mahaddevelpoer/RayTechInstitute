## Accuracy and source review

The guide distinguishes input from output at component/function level, including touchscreens, headsets, terminals and multifunction equipment. It separates recording from recognition, scanning from OCR, OCR from OMR/MICR, biometric verification from identification, displayed output from saved work, and resolution from physical size/refresh/response time. The common LED-monitor label is explained as LED-backlit LCD without confusing it with OLED/direct-view LED. Printer coverage separates liquid ink, toner, direct thermal and thermal transfer; traditional pen plotters are not equated with every modern large-format inkjet. Basic demonstrations use equipment settings and external observation.

References include PBTE, EIZO, Epson, Zebra, Adobe and NIST. Manufacturer sources are used for technology explanations rather than unsupported model rankings or purchasing promises. The original question bank includes explanations and four distinct options; answer positions are deterministically shuffled and balanced, 20 per letter.
