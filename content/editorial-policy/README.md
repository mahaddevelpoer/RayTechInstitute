# Article policy effective 27 November 2026

All articles in legacy-noindex.json stay noindex, follow, including after edits. Preserve IDs and slugs. Do not delete this frozen list or infer index eligibility from publication dates. These pages remain available and crawlable but are excluded from the sitemap.

New articles must concern Pakistan education or international educational updates. How/why/who wording does not make unrelated news educational. No new general trend, sports, entertainment, political or business reporting.

Before publication, review the actual educational relevance, factual claims and issuing institution's primary source. Set seo.educationScope to pakistan-education or global-education and record checked HTTPS references in seo.sources. Use date-sheets, admissions, career-guides or education categories. This is a human editorial declaration, not automatic source verification. Never fabricate review or attribution. Unreviewed or out-of-scope posts remain noindex. Existing source-review checks still apply.

Class notes, MCQs, past papers, syllabuses and other academic resource routes keep their existing indexing. A syllabus article under /blogs/ is an article and follows the legacy rule; its separate academic resource page remains eligible.
